A simple guide to how it all works

This guide explains, in everyday language, how guests book and stay with us, and how the team runs the villas from one place. It covers Villa Maaya, Villa Praana and Villa Nirvaana, each with four bedrooms.

The big picture

The public website is where people discover us. This system is everything that happens after someone shows interest: the booking, the payment, the stay itself, and the goodbye.

Two ways to sell a villa

Whole villa — one group takes the entire villa and all four bedrooms. When it is booked, none of its rooms can be sold to anyone else.
Split into rooms — each bedroom (A-1, B-1, C-1, D-1) is sold on its own, so different guests can share a villa. Booking Room A leaves the other rooms free.
The system never lets the same bed be sold twice. If dates overlap with an existing booking for that villa or room, it refuses to save and tells you which booking is in the way.
Part 1 · For guests

How a new guest comes on board

From first click to "you're confirmed" — usually a few minutes for the guest, and one click for the front desk.

Step by step

  1. Choose a stay. The guest picks a villa (or individual rooms when the villa is split), dates and number of adults and children. Unavailable dates simply can't be chosen.
  2. Create an account with name, email, phone and a password. Every new sign-up is a guest account — nobody can make themselves an admin.
  3. See the booking voucher. It shows the full price breakdown: nightly rate × nights, weekend or season extras, food, add-ons, discount, GST, and the total.
  4. Pay using the UPI ID or bank details shown on the payment screen.
  5. Upload the receipt — attach the screenshot, type the amount and the transaction (UTR) number, and submit. Status changes to Payment Uploaded.
  6. Wait for approval. The front desk compares the receipt with the booking. If it's approved the booking becomes Confirmed; if not, the guest is told the exact reason (wrong amount, unreadable, duplicate, wrong account, etc.) and can upload again.
Booked by phone or WhatsApp? The front desk creates the booking for you. You can then sign in with the same email or phone number and your stay will already be waiting in your portal.
Dates sold out? Guests can join the waiting list. If a booking is cancelled and those dates free up, the team is reminded to offer them to the next person in line.

Part 2 · For the owner, manager and front desk

Running the day

Start every morning on the Dashboard. It answers: who is arriving, who is leaving, who is staying, what money is waiting, and what needs attention.

A typical morning

  1. Dashboard — read today's arrivals and departures, and the Needs attention list (payments to check, unpaid arrivals, urgent requests, late check-outs).
  2. Payments queue — open each uploaded receipt beside its booking, compare the amount, then Approve or Reject with a reason. A warning shows if the amounts don't match.
  3. Front desk — check guests in and out as they arrive and leave.
  4. Kitchen & requests — keep food orders and guest requests moving.
  5. Housekeeping — mark rooms clean so they can be sold again.

Taking a booking by phone

  1. Go to Bookings → New booking.
  2. Choose the source (phone, WhatsApp, Goibibo, walk-in, referral…).
  3. Pick the villa. If it's split into rooms, pick the rooms.
  4. Enter dates, guests and the guest's details. Existing guests are found by phone or email.
  5. Set rate, discount, tax and any advance received.
  6. Save. If the dates clash with another booking, it is blocked and the clashing booking is named.

The life of a booking

Each colour and label means the same thing everywhere in the system. Status is always written as a word, never shown by colour alone. Every booking keeps a timeline of what happened and who did it, plus internal notes the guest never sees.

Seeing the whole property

The Calendar shows a month grid and a timeline with all three villas — and each room when a villa is split. Click a booking to open it; click an empty date to start a new booking there. The Villas page is where you switch a villa between Whole villa and Split into rooms, and update rates, check-in times, amenities and the Wi-Fi password.

For kitchen and housekeeping staff

Staff sign in and see only their own queue — the kitchen sees food orders, housekeeping sees rooms and requests. Pick up the next job, move it forward, mark it done. Nothing else to learn.

Golden rules

  • A room or villa can never be booked twice for the same nights.
  • A whole-villa booking blocks all four of its rooms.
  • A booking is only Confirmed after a payment is approved.
  • Rejecting a payment or cancelling a booking always asks "are you sure?" and records the reason.
  • Billed food joins the guest's bill automatically.
  • Guests only ever see their own stay.

Part 3 · For the owner

Connecting Goibibo, Booking.com, Airbnb and other OTAs

Online travel agencies don't let a small property connect to them directly. They connect through a certified channel manager. The CRM uses Channex: OTA bookings arrive in the CRM on their own, and every booking, move or cancellation in the CRM closes or reopens those dates on every OTA.

One-time setup (about an hour)

  1. Create a Channex account: staging.channex.io (sandbox) first. Add the property (INR, Asia/Kolkata). Create a room type for each villa sold whole, or one per bedroom for a villa sold room by room, plus one rate plan each.
  2. Connect each OTA inside Channex, from your own OTA accounts. Goibibo/MMT: add the channel in Channex and ask your MMT market manager (or the InGo-MMT extranet) to map the property to Channex. Booking.com: Extranet → Account → Connectivity provider → Channex. Airbnb: sign in through Airbnb's consent screen inside Channex. Then map each OTA room to a Channex room type.
  3. Create an API key: Channex → Profile → API keys → Create ("Sanctuary CRM").
  4. In the CRM: Settings → Channels. Follow the five steps on screen: environment and property ID, then paste the API key, map villas/rooms to room type IDs, Test connection and Register webhook, and finally turn on sync.
  5. Prove it works: make a test booking in Channex staging. It appears in Bookings marked "via …". Block dates in the CRM, and Channex shows 0 availability. Then switch to Production with a production key.

Security. The API key goes straight into an encrypted vault on the server. It is never stored in the browser, never shown again (only its last four characters) and only the owner can change it. The webhook is protected by a secret only the server knows, and every incoming booking is re-fetched from Channex rather than trusted. We never ask for OTA passwords. If a key leaks or someone leaves, delete it in Channex and paste a new one.

When something needs attention

  • Unmapped: an OTA sold a room type the CRM doesn't know. Add it to the mapping and enter that booking by hand.
  • Conflict: those nights were already held in the CRM. The CRM never double-books: it refuses the OTA booking and alerts you. Move or cancel one stay.
  • Nothing arriving: Re-register webhook, then Sync now. It collects anything missed.

No channel manager yet? Airbnb and Booking.com can swap calendar (.ics) links as a stopgap. These carry dates only and update every few hours. Goibibo doesn't support it.
